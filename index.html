✨ The 3 Easy Steps to Answer Science Questions

C

CLAIM = Your Answer

Write 1 short sentence that answers the question directly.

Tip: Choose an option when the question asks you to choose between things.
E

EVIDENCE = The Clue

What did you see or measure in the picture or table?

Tip: Look out for Key words or phrases in the question, diagrams, observationsm tables or graphs.
R

REASONING = Science Rule

Explain WHY your clue proves your answer using a Science fact.

Example: "Living things need air, food, and water to survive."
